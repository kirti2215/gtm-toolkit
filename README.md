# gtm-toolkit
Open-source GTM skills for Claude. Regional campaigns, battle cards, messaging frameworks, one skill per workflow, built to be repurposed.

For PMMs, GTM leaders, and sales teams who want AI that actually 
understands go-to-market. A growing collection of skills covering 
regional campaigns, messaging, competitive intel, and sales enablement 
— one skill per workflow, built to be repurposed.

## Skills

### 🌎 GTM Regional Campaign Builder
Drop in your product, region, industry, and audience — get a full 
campaign brief in minutes. Covers regional context, messaging framework, 
business case, target accounts, and success metrics. Adapts for 
executive buyers, technical buyers, or internal sales teams.

**Install:** Download `gtm-regional-campaign-builder.skill` and add 
it to your Cowork or Claude Code plugins.
