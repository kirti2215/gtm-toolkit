# Product Launch Planner — Example Input

> Fictionalized scenario used for testing. Company names, product names, customers, and regulatory details are illustrative only.

---

## 1. Product

**Product name and one-line description**

NovaBuild — a managed foundation model platform that enables enterprises to build, deploy, and govern AI applications without managing underlying infrastructure.

**Key capabilities**

- Managed access to multiple foundation models
- Built-in knowledge base connectors
- Agent orchestration
- Prompt guardrails
- Regional data residency controls
- Enterprise identity integration
- Usage monitoring and governance

**What the product does not do**

- Does not train foundation models from scratch
- Does not replace existing data warehouses
- Does not provide pre-built industry applications
- Does not eliminate customer responsibility for application governance

## 4. Market Timing

**Why is this launch happening now?**

A new regional data resilience framework has introduced clearer expectations for AI workloads processing sensitive financial data. Organizations reviewing their AI strategy are reassessing infrastructure decisions to ensure future deployments align with evolving regulatory expectations.

NovaBuild recently became available in the Singapore region with capabilities designed to support these deployment requirements.

**What was true 12 months ago that is different today?**

Many organizations were experimenting with AI through pilot projects while regulatory expectations were still evolving. Today, financial institutions are shifting from experimentation toward production deployments with greater emphasis on governance, operational controls, and regional data handling.

**What happens if the company does not launch now?**

Competitors may establish themselves as the default production platform before customers revisit their AI architecture decisions, making future displacement significantly more difficult.
