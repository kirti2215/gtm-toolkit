# Competitive Battle Card — Example Input

> Fictionalized scenario used for testing. Company names, product names, and deal details are illustrative only.

---

## 1. Market & Product

**Your product or solution**  
EnterpriseDB Pro, a relational database platform

**Target industry or vertical**  
Fintech / regulated payments

**Primary use case**  
Core transaction processing for a payments platform with strict reliability, auditability, and compliance requirements

---

## 2. Competitor

**Competitor name**  
FlexData, a document database with a flexible schema

**Why is the competitor in this deal?**  
Developer or technical-team recommendation.

The engineering team recommended FlexData because several team members used it at a previous startup and prefer its query model, flexible schema, and developer experience for early-stage iteration.

**What does the competitor genuinely do better?**  
- Faster initial setup
- Flexible schema during early development
- Strong developer familiarity and preference
- Lower perceived friction for rapid iteration

---

## 3. Buyer & Stakeholders

**Primary buyer or decision-maker**  
VP of Engineering

**Technical evaluator**  
Two to three senior engineers who recommended FlexData

**Economic buyer**  
CFO and Head of Infrastructure

**Internal champion**  
None identified

**Potential detractors**  
The engineering team, which currently has a strong preference for FlexData

---

## 4. Deal Context

**Deal stage**  
Active evaluation

**Timeline to decision**  
Architecture decision expected within six weeks

**Known decision criteria**  
- Developer experience
- Scalability
- Total cost
- Compliance readiness
- Operational reliability

**Known objections or concerns**  
> “Our engineers do not want to deal with rigid schemas at this stage.”

The team is concerned that a relational model may slow early development and reduce flexibility as the product evolves.

## 5. Competitive Positioning

**Where do you genuinely win?**  
- ACID transactions and strong consistency guarantees
- Mature capabilities for regulated transaction processing
- Production-grade reliability at scale

- Stronger fit for workloads where transaction integrity and operational accountability are critical

**Where does the competitor genuinely win?**  
- Developer experience
- Schema flexibility
- Speed of early development
- Existing familiarity within the engineering team

None identified
