## 3. Account Context

**Current environment**

MeridianBank currently operates a self-managed NoSQL database cluster supporting customer transaction workloads.

The platform is maintained by a small database operations team.

The environment has experienced multiple service interruptions requiring manual failover and operational intervention.

**Known business initiatives**

- Enterprise cloud migration
- Operational resilience improvement program
- Infrastructure modernization
- Reduction of operational support costs

**Relationship status**

Active evaluation

**Deal stage**

Evaluation

**Known buyer objections**

- Concern about vendor lock-in
- Existing team has strong expertise with the current platform
- Uncertainty that managed infrastructure justifies the additional cost
- Migration effort may outweigh operational benefits

**Competitive situation**

Primary competitor is the existing self-managed database platform.

Alternative option is improving the current environment rather than replacing it.

**Why is the competitor in the deal?**

The incumbent platform is well understood by the engineering team, already integrated into production systems, and perceived as less disruptive than migration.

---

## 4. Business Drivers

**Why is this deal happening now?**

The bank is reviewing critical infrastructure following several operational incidents while simultaneously progressing a broader cloud modernization program.

Leadership wants to reduce operational complexity before expanding customer-facing digital services.

**What happens if the customer does nothing?**

- Continued operational overhead
- Higher infrastructure support costs
- Increased engineering time spent maintaining database operations
- Greater risk of future service interruptions
- Cloud modernization timeline slows

**Success criteria**

- Improve operational reliability
- Reduce database administration effort
- Minimize migration risk
- Support long-term cloud strategy
- Maintain business continuity during migration

## 6. Evidence

**Reference customers**

CoastalPay (fictionalized)

UK-based digital payments company that migrated from a self-managed NoSQL platform to NovaSphere DB.

**Verified product evidence**

- 99.999% availability SLA
- Zero-RPO replication
- Automated failover
- Managed backups
- Enterprise security certifications

**Customer or field signals**

- Infrastructure leaders increasingly prioritize operational simplicity over managing database infrastructure internally.
- Existing customers report faster platform modernization after removing database operations from engineering teams.
- Sellers consistently encounter concerns around migration effort and vendor lock-in.

**Assumptions to validate**

- Operational resilience is a higher executive priority than infrastructure cost.
- Migration effort is acceptable if operational burden decreases.
- Executive stakeholders are willing to prioritize long-term operational improvements over short-term migration costs.

---

## 7. Sales Readiness

**Known seller challenges**

Conversations begin with database features instead of customer business priorities.

Sellers struggle to connect infrastructure modernization with executive business outcomes.

**Known technical-team challenges**

Solutions Architects need stronger migration guidance, architecture diagrams, and operational resilience examples.

**Current messaging problem**

Current messaging focuses on database performance and managed services.

Customers care more about reducing operational risk and supporting long-term modernization initiatives.

**Desired seller behavior**

Lead with the customer's business priorities, operational challenges, and modernization goals before discussing product capabilities.
